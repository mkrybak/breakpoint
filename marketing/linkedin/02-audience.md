## Realistic targets

| | Reactions |
|---|---|
| Baseline today | ~1 |
| Post 1 | 20–40 (my career-milestone level) |
| A good post | 50–100 |
| A breakout | 200+, mostly from people who don't follow me |

"Viral" in month one is a long shot. Aim: one breakout within 6 weeks, plus steady growth in profile views and recruiter visits. Judge formats by **comments**, not reactions.
