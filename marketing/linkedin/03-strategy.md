# 03 — Strategy

Built from [[01-research]] (what works) and [[02-audience]] (what's realistic for me).

## Rules

1. **One post a week, Thursday ~7–8am PT.** Catches Vancouver's morning and Ukraine's evening. Avoid BC statutory holidays (e.g. Remembrance Day, Nov 11).
2. **Struggle and bugs before announcements.** Peers' complaint posts beat their launch posts.
3. **Every post gets a visual.** GIF or screenshot from the app.
4. **Plain English, short sentences.** Many readers aren't native speakers.
5. **Link only in the first comment:** https://breakmydesign.com/
6. **No hashtags, no 𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱, no emoji bullets, no "Agree?".**
7. **Only real facts.** Every number comes from the repo or real usage. Anything personal I haven't actually lived gets cut, never invented.
8. **Hello Interview is the framework I follow, never a partner.**

## Week 0 warm-up

See [[W0-warmup]]. LinkedIn decides reach in the first hour, mostly from people I've recently interacted with. The warm-up builds that pool.

## First-hour routine (every post)

- [ ] Post, then immediately add the first comment with the link
- [ ] Stay around for 60 minutes and reply to every comment with a real sentence, not "Thanks!"
- [ ] Leave 3–5 real comments on other people's posts during that hour
- [ ] Don't edit the post in the first hour

## Pre-post checklist

- [ ] Read it out loud; cut any line I wouldn't say to a colleague
- [ ] All [brackets] filled or deleted
- [ ] Visual attached and readable on a phone
- [ ] No hashtags, no link in body
- [ ] Numbers double-checked against the repo

## After week 3: review

Compare W1–W3 in [[tracker]]. Double down on whichever format got the most **comments**. If the puzzle wins, make W5 a puzzle too. If the bug story wins, add another "the simulator was wrong" post.

## Optional

- **Ukrainian-language post** ([[optional-ukrainian-puzzle]]): about a quarter of my audience, and Ukrainian posts perform as well as English ones in my network.
- **AI workflow post** ([[optional-ai-workflow]]): only if rewritten around one concrete moment; generic AI takes get 5–8 reactions in my network.
- **Vancouver meetups**: offer a 5-minute demo; post about it afterwards (a real event = a real story).
- **Comment on bigger creators** (Evan King, Neo Kim, Anton Martyniuk) with real substance, never "check out my tool".
