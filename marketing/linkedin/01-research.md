## The formula

1. **Hook, 1–2 short lines**: a tension the reader has felt, about them, not me.
2. **One real first-person moment** with a specific detail or number.
3. **One insight**, not ten.
4. **Proof**: a GIF or screenshot. My feed is all text and PDFs; a design turning red stands out.
5. **Quiet ending**: a dry line or an honest open question. Never "Agree?".
6. **Never**: hashtags, links in the body (first comment instead), 𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱 unicode, emoji bullets, "game-changer", "let that sink in", em-dashes everywhere (reads as AI).

## Why this series means something

- **For readers**: nearly every engineer fears the system design interview, and it gives zero feedback: you draw boxes and hope. BreakMyDesign *shows* a design break. Each post is one real lesson people can watch happen.
- **It hits the two hottest topics in my feed**: AI-assisted building and interview prep.
- **For me**: proof of work that matches my headline (backend, distributed systems, payments), and it gives a dormant account a clear theme.
